QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What is PRIZEN?

A gaming and community project with announcements and community activities.

How do I join?

Tap any Join Discord button. If the invitation is invalid, create a new invite in your Discord server and update the link in this file.

Are rewards guaranteed?

No. Check official event details, eligibility, and terms. This page does not promise prizes.

Does the website run the bot?

No. A Discord bot needs its own application and running host. Never publish your bot token in website code.

Will PRIZEN ask for my password?

Never share your Discord password, account token, or recovery codes with a bot or another user.
